Fourmation is a consultancy of senior architects. We work where technology choices have real consequences — across systems, teams, cloud platforms and organisations.

Our work can begin with a strategic direction, a troublesome flow or a codebase that needs to move forward. Whatever the size, it ends with something running in production.

We analyse, design, build and improve — and take responsibility from the first decision to production.

Our work

From concrete problems to larger systems

Integration & APIs

We design and build what makes systems and organisations work together: APIs, messaging, event streaming and integration platforms. With clear ownership, well-designed contracts and operations built into the architecture.

Cloud native & platforms

We design platforms and distributed systems for real production environments. Kubernetes, containers, automation, observability and resilience — designed for the organisation that will operate them.

Software engineering

We build services, products and interfaces when architecture needs to become working code. Backend, frontend and everything in between — built for clarity, longevity and change.

Architecture & technical leadership

We take lead architect and technical advisory roles where many systems, teams and decisions need to align. Discovery, target architecture and technology choices — plus hands-on leadership, code and concrete work throughout delivery.

AI in real systems

Good AI starts with usable data

Your organisation's data and services are the foundation for AI that creates real value.

We make the right information available, build the services around it and connect AI to existing systems and processes. Security, evaluation, traceability and operations are part of the architecture from the start.

Who we are

Fourmation is shaped by the people who build it. This is what we stand for, why we enjoy working together and what you can expect from a consultant who enters your team.

Freedom through friendship

Even though we are unique in our own ways, we are united by one vividly strong dream: freedom. Freedom to be and do what we want — enabled by friendship.

With admiration, curiosity and love we challenge one another from our different points of view. It is when we discuss that we learn and grow closer. Together we become a solid unit, flying through life in a beautiful, free Fourmation — ready to solve any challenge.

Solvers by birth

We love solving problems, bugs and headaches. We are forward-leaning solvers who share every discovery and insight — with each other and with our customers.

By looking at the bigger picture we make complexity understandable, and together we find a way to deliver even better than expected.

Proud nerds

With curiosity about life and its obscurities we may come across as a slightly nerdy bunch. Perhaps because we love digging deep into wildly different topics.

That is what true nerdiness is all about, right?

We are proud of it — equipped with an eagle eye for detail and an eagerness to share what we know, with everyone.

Word keepers, trust seekers

Trust is everything. We cherish every adventure, whether the challenge is brand new or well known.

Whatever the task, we stand by our word and deliver on our promises.

More than Fourmation
